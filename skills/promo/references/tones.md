# Tones

Presets set pacing, type and copy voice. Freeform direction refines or overrides ("like a friend showing you a trick", "luxury skincare").

| Tone | Feel | Pacing | Type and look | Copy voice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `native` (default for reels) | Looks like a person posted it | Fast cuts (1–2 s), jump into the demo | Big bold sans captions with a soft box, no gradients, raw app footage full screen | Casual, second person, lowercase allowed: "wait till you see the after" |
| `premium` | Calm, expensive, confident | Long holds, slow push-ins, 3–4 beats | Serif headline + light sans, lots of space, brand color as accent only | Short, declarative: "Your best shot. Fixed." |
| `app-store` | Clear, feature-forward, polished | Even beats, smooth slides | Brand typeface, device frame, clean background | Benefit + feature: "Track every receipt in one tap" |
| `before-after` | The transformation is the whole ad | Hold before, reveal, hold after, repeat with a second example | Minimal text, labels "Before" / "After", slider or dissolve | One line: "Same photo. One tap." |
| `problem-solution` | Relatable pain, instant fix | Problem 2–3 s → fix → result | Native captions, emoji-free unless the brand uses them | Viewer's words for the pain, then the fix |
| `playful` | Light, fun, a bit cheeky | Bouncy entrances, quick beats | Rounded type, bright accent, stickers-style labels | Puns allowed if they come from the feature |

Choose `native` or `problem-solution` for TikTok/Reels, `app-store` for the preview and screenshots, `premium` or `before-after` for photo, fashion and lifestyle apps. Every tone follows the creative laws in `SKILL.md`.
